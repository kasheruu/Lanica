About Lanica

Quality Furniture Since 2018 • 5-Star Rated Shop

Basta't Tatak Lanica, Quality!

Lanica's Sofa Sala Set takes pride in providing premium quality furniture with upgraded size versions of our products. Since 2017, we've been serving Laguna and Calamba with exceptional sofa sets and sala furniture that combines comfort, durability, and modern design.

Our commitment to excellence has earned us a 5-star rating with zero bad feedbacks. We believe that quality furniture should be accessible to every Filipino home, which is why we offer upgraded sizes and premium designs at competitive prices.
